<h1>KATEŘINA JIČINSK&Aacute;</h1>
<h2>(In)sensitivity</h2>
<p><em>Photography Studio</em><br><br><strong>You describe the installation as an &ldquo;ecosystem of sensitivity&rdquo; that reacts&nbsp;to humidity, temperature, and the viewer&rsquo;s presence. How important was&nbsp;it for you that the work would not be just a stable object, but something&nbsp;vulnerable and changeable?</strong><br>I find it very similar to interpersonal relationships. While the installation is&nbsp;stable within the space, it was essential for me to allow room for external&nbsp;factors that would help shape and transform it.</p>
<p><strong>In your text, you consider paper a second skin, as it bears the marks of&nbsp;time, and reacts both to its surrounding conditions and to touch. Do you&nbsp;think paper and skin can be equivalent media for sensitivity?</strong><br>I was reminded of the paper-thin skin that grandmothers often have. I think&nbsp;they are indeed very similar insofar as both are layered, vulnerable, and&nbsp;carry their own stories.</p>
<p><strong>You chose lipstick as your medium. It is an object that is highly charged,&nbsp;both culturally and in terms of gender. I am curious what drew you to it. Is it&nbsp;the intimacy and connection to the body, or the social meanings it carries?</strong><br>Both aspects are dear to me and I feel they are part of it, but during the&nbsp;process itself, the question of intimacy and the body emerged as the more&nbsp;important one.</p>
