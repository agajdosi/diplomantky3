<h1>Hana Kok&scaron;alov&aacute;</h1>
<h2>Terra Incognita</h2>
<p>Environment Studio</p>
<p>The work describes the artistic research dedicated to the interior of planet Earth, and consists of narrative analysis of scientific and fictional texts pertaining to this environment. It provides a basis for the authors&rsquo; own literary experiments in speculative fiction, attempting to minimize human subjectivity in storytelling. The research includes somatic exploration inspired by shifts of sensory perception while in the underground environment and develops this into a performative, collaborative, movement-based practice.&nbsp;</p>
