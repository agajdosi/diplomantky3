<h1>Barbora Novotn&aacute;</h1>
<h2>Pop&ndash;Up, What Else?</h2>
<p>Game Media Studio<br><br>Through the concept of a pop-up book, the work explores children&rsquo;s literature at the borders between horror and fairytale. Apart from the book&rsquo;s interactivity and content, the artist considers the book as an object or toy which functions even after the initial fascination has worn off. The outcome is an original pop-up book composed of a series of movable pages, showing their thematic and technical design, illustration and final production. It aims to expand the reader&rsquo;s experience through visual and haptic play.</p>
