<h1>Johana Kopeck&aacute;</h1>
<h2>Frame</h2>
<p>Product Design Studio</p>
<p>The work establishes a dialogue between cyclist and bicycle, searching for a way to describe the latent feelings of riding a bicycle. The artist focuses on the frame (the bike&rsquo;s centre), which carries its structural essence as well as the rider&rsquo;s personal story. Through visual associations, inspiration found in nature, material experiments and textiles, it explores how emotions, touch, and identity enter the cyclist&rsquo;s world. The outcome is a woven object &ndash; a natural part of the frame and a functional, fragile layer emphasizing the bike&rsquo;s character and its owner&rsquo;s relationship to it.</p>
