<h1>Anežka Součkov&aacute;</h1>
<h2>The Virgin, the Bitch, the Witch</h2>
<p>Intermedia Studio</p>
<p>The work reflects on the experience of ageing in a female body aged 20&ndash;30 within the context of late-capitalist patriarchal society. The short film uses strong symbolic language to ponder the ancient question of time and the search for meaning through a feminist survey of pressures, burdens, and feelings of being dispensable. Through text, filmic language and original music, it weaves together situations in which mud plays a central role.</p>
