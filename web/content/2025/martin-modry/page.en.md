<h2>The Oracle as a Prototype of a Game Without Rules</h2>
<p>Game Media Studio<br><br>The work explores digital divination tools and their specific features in the online environment, resulting in the project spiritualnikancelar.cz. Combining metaphysics and interactivity, it reflects on the relationship between tradition and algorithm. By using evolutionary algorithms, collective participation and database interactions, the work thematizes randomness, interpretation, collective memory and identity in the digital age.</p>
