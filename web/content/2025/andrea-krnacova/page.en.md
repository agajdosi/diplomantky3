<h1>Andrea Krn&aacute;čov&aacute;</h1>
<h2>NeSR</h2>
<p>Sculpture Studio</p>
<p>The work offers a critical reaction to the political situation in Slovakia. By means of small-scale figurative sculptural scenes, the artist reflects on the steps taken by the current government. It uses the language of caricature and satire, connecting art with a civic position, showing the ongoing social tension and absurdity of its political decisions.</p>
