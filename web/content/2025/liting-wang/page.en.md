<h1>Liting Wang</h1>
<h2>Narrative and Emotional Expression in Painting as a Storytelling Tool&nbsp;</h2>
<p>Fine Art and Design<br><br>The work, whose alternate title is Women Beyond Definition, comprises a series of images and a video which explore the trajectory of the female subject from social discipline towards the awakening of female subjectivity and autonomy. The diversity of formats and media &ndash; from traditional oil paintings to an interdisciplinary video &ndash; reflects the topic as well as the artist&rsquo;s expressive style. The project explores the possibility of female emancipation and presents both a personal and creative breakthrough.</p>
