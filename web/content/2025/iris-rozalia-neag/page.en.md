<p>Iris-Rozalia Neag<br><br>Mansion</p>
<p>Fine Art and Design</p>
<p>The work features two playable female 3D characters and is set in Transylvania in the late 18th and the 20th centuries. It connects Gothic aesthetics, vampire folklore and feminist perspectives, aiming to create visually striking characters which reflect the female experience and oppression. The results feature fully textured game models with original music, and explore the cultural context and its treatment of gender roles across time.</p>
