<h1>Julie Dudov&aacute;</h1>
<h2>Dialogue as a (Core) Design Practice</h2>
<p>Graphic Design Studio 1</p>
<p>The work addresses the topic of dialogue and its importance in the academic environment and beyond. It was developed as a course for design students at FFA BUT, accompanied by other materials which propose possible uses of its outputs beyond the course itself. The work includes personal experience from publishing, as well as data gained through surveys. It emphasizes the privileges and roles defined by the social structures &nbsp;which we inhabit, and which impact our positions and critical thinking.</p>
