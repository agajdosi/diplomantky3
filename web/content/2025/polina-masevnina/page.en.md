<h1>Polina Masevnina</h1>
<h2>It Is Indeed a Dance</h2>
<p>Body Design Studio&nbsp;<br><br>Using the language of hybrid prose, conceptual metaphor and autotheory, the work explores the contemporary notions of love, intimacy and interpersonal encounters. It connects psychological concepts with the cultural discourses of love, post-love, and the conscious experience of relationships. Combining design, text, and space, as well as experimental publications in the form of a zine, it connects temporary media, artistic research, and personal experience as a means of reflecting on love today.</p>
