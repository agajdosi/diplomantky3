<h1>Aneta Chalabalov&aacute;</h1>
<h2>Lost and Found</h2>
<p>Spatial Design Studio</p>
<p>The work consists of a prototype for a first-person digital game with survival and adventure features. It was produced for the Android platform and made in the Unity engine. The game is based on a synthesis of sound work &ndash; while it returns to more traditional techniques, it experiments and takes them further. The artist&rsquo;s appreciation for the traditional medium is transformed into themes of the body which she attempts to connect functionally by means of sound creation.</p>
