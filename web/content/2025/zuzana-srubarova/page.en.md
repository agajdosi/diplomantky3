<h1>Zuzana &Scaron;rubařov&aacute;</h1>
<h2>In Them</h2>
<p>Sculpture Studio<br><br>The work explores the topic of comfort, which the artist paradoxically finds in confrontation with inner anxiety. Using a spatial installation and audio recordings of dialogues, she explores the ways in which people close to her resolve their inner turmoil. The project is based on her personal experience and opens a shared space for reflecting conscious and unconscious layers of experience, creating a sensitive dialogue between intimacy, memory and collective experience.</p>
