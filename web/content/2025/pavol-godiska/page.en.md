<h1>Pavol Godi&scaron;ka</h1>
<h2>Desire, I Want to Turn into You</h2>
<p>Intermedia Studio</p>
<p>The graduate work focuses on the artist&rsquo;s personal desires &ndash; particularly the materialist and physical ambitions formed around ideas of success, status and the ideal body. These desires are explored in two artistic objects addressing the tension between the fascination with consumerism and criticism of it. The material composing the objects thematizes the distinction between the accessible and inaccessible, and emphasizes the contrast between the mundane and the ideal.</p>
