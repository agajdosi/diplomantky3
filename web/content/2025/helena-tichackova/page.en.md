<h2>Gap</h2>
<p>Painting Studio 1<br><br>The work focuses on the theme of a home &ldquo;under construction&rdquo; and the technology of iconography. It is inspired by a study of iconography during its golden age in the 14th&ndash;15th century, as well as by theological texts. It emphasizes preparation and expressive means, and the resulting series of paintings connects historical principles and a personal search for place, identity and closeness.</p>
