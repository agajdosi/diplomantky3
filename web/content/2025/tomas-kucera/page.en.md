<h1>Tom&aacute;&scaron; Kučera</h1>
<h2>Threshold</h2>
<p>Painting Studio 1</p>
<p>The work focuses on the topic of overstimulation and disorientation in the contemporary world. The series of mid-size oil paintings uses the principle of automation, particularly the decal technique and its subsequent processing. The work is accompanied by a textual part &ndash; a painters&rsquo; diary &ndash; reflecting the motivation for the individual works&rsquo; creation.</p>
