<h1>Katar&iacute;na Maro&scaron;iov&aacute;</h1>
<h2>Between Material and Technology&nbsp;</h2>
<p>Spatial Design Studio<br><br>In the context of technology, new materialism and post-anthropocentric thinking, the work explores potato starch as a valid material. Rather than designing a final product, it researches the temporality and relationships between material substances. Using experimentation as a research tool, the artist creates a fiction about a material. The work straddles the border between a quasi-scientific and an artistic approach, striving for a degree of realism while exploring the potential use of starch in architecture.</p>
