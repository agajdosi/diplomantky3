<h1>Barbora Sap&aacute;kov&aacute;</h1>
<h2>Landscape in the Landscape</h2>
<p>Painting Studio 1</p>
<p>The work reflects on the artist&rsquo;s relationship to nature, exploring the connection of painting and its installation in the wild. It explores the possibility of the painting&rsquo;s dialogue with its surroundings and attempts to find its harmonious relationship. The artist develops the thoughts of David Abram&rsquo;s book Spell of the Sensuous, in which he explores the harmonious relationship between people and nature. She also connects the work with the context of land art and its relation to painting installations in the countryside.</p>
