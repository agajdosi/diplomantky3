<h1>Gregor Hra&scaron;ko</h1>
<h2>Traumatic Event and the Gloom of Death from Elite Sport as a Foundation for Self-Determination in the Present</h2>
<p>Intermedia Studio</p>
<p>Fight Till Death is a multimedia work reflecting the artist&rsquo;s experiences in elite sports and their subculture. The title, inspired by the band Slayer, refers to the extreme motivation to &lsquo;fight to the death&rsquo;. &nbsp;Through video, drawing and music, the artist creates an installation reminiscent of a metal concert. Apart from the collage and ink drawings, music also forms part of the visuals. A central motif is the artificial face &ndash; a mask which serves to hide the darker aspects of elite sports.</p>
