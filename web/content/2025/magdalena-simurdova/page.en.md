<h1>Magdalena &Scaron;imurdov&aacute;</h1>
<h2>Seed, Hide and Seek: Echoes of Presence</h2>
<p>Spatial Design Studio</p>
<p>The work explores the posthuman world, through a spatial installation where organic and civilizational materials create new forms of non-human entities. The beings are not representations but rather active actors in their environment. The project is based on the principles of new materialism and posthumanism, exploring material agency and relationality.Through dissolving binaries, it expands the understanding of subjectivity beyond the limits of the human.</p>
