<h1>Karol Toman</h1>
<h2>Dystransmorphosis</h2>
<p>Video Studio<br><br>The work reflects the personal path and transition of a transgender man by means of a video installation and glass objects. Apart from the transformations of the body and identity, it also addresses the everyday struggle against gender dysphoria. The project connects documentary approaches, dialogue and the visual symbolism of fragility and strength, aiming to familiarize the audience with the transgender experience and open space for sensitive discussion about various gender identities, their social perception and expectations.&nbsp;</p>
