<h1>Maty&aacute;&scaron; Mrkus</h1>
<h2>3D Asset Creation for Digital Games</h2>
<p>Game Media Studio<br><br>The work focuses on the process of constructing fully functional 3D assets for digital games. It explores the tensions between craftsmanship and fine art, and attempts to offer a tool for orienting oneself in the industry&rsquo;s ecosystem. The results consist of models and documentation of the development process, including the mistakes made, and can thus serve as a source of information to facilitate the transition from the school environment to the industry of digital games and 3D design.</p>
