<h1>Jan Mikač</h1>
<h2>Stick and Stone Studio</h2>
<p>Graphic Design Studio 1<br><br>The work maps the development of the Stick and Stone Studio &ndash; a space for design, research and the production of audio systems/technologies. Based on his experience and theoretical know-how gained as part of the agdx collective, the author formulates a method for the studio&rsquo;s future practice. The outcome is the studio&rsquo;s visual identity, web presentation, textual frame, as well as a multifunctional speaker, and establishes it on the border between design, craft and sound.</p>
