<h1>Vendula Petrov&aacute;</h1>
<h2>Hairo</h2>
<p>Sculpture Studio<br><br>The work is a spatial installation made of water-soluble materials, and it explores the topic of farewells and the acceptance of loss &ndash; both of place and of loved ones. It features a performance in which the artist waters a model of her bedroom as a metaphor for purification and transience. Connecting voice, body and space in ritual reconciliation, it offers an empathetic view into the universal experience of loss, transformation and acceptance &ndash; an inevitable and creative part of our personal growth.</p>
