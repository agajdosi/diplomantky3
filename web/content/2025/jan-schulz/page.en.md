<h1>Jan Schulz</h1>
<h2>The Gotofuturismus</h2>
<p>Painting Studio 3<br><br>Using the format of a multimedia installation, the work presents the original gotofuturism &ndash; a critical reflection on the polarization of society and the negative impacts of technological progress. The artist works with vertical and horizontal vectors of ideologies and emphasizes the importance of the present moment. Developing on the theories of John Ruskin and Guy Debord, and futurist manifestos, the objects connect the concept with visual and critical commentary.</p>
