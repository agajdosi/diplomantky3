<h1>&Scaron;&aacute;rka Stejskalov&aacute;</h1>
<h2>Glasshouse</h2>
<p>Sculpture Studio</p>
<p>The work focuses on the relationship between people and nature, connecting aesthetic and contemplative experience with the topic of memory and vernacular architecture. The result is a landscape observatory &ndash; a visually striking object made of jars found in a gardening colony &ndash; as well as a zine mapping the origins and story of the jars. The project reflects on the anthropocentric perspective and explores responsibility towards the natural environment.</p>
