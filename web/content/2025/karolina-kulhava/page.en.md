<h1>Karol&iacute;na Kulhav&aacute;</h1>
<h2>Without a Roof</h2>
<p>Painting Studio 2</p>
<p>The artistic project was undertaken in collaboration with the clients of a Brno-based NGO and focuses on the topic of homelessness, exploring artistic activity as a means of establishing relationships and sharing. The project attempts to reflect and shift the discourse towards dialogue and the role of the artist as a mediator between personal experience and the social context. It offers a critical reflection of the possibilities and limits of engaged art.</p>
