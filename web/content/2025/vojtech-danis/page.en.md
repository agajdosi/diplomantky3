<h1>Vojtěch Dani&scaron;</h1>
<h2>Big Little Things</h2>
<p>Drawing and Printmaking Studio</p>
<p>The graduate work is a follow-up to a BA project in which the author attempted to reflect on his inner child. He puts himself in the position of narrator and references certain symbols and motifs from fairy tales. This chapter also explores particular memories while not losing sight of his present &ldquo;self.&rdquo; The installation consciously works with the mindset and perception of an adult, yet provides opportunity to see the world through a child&rsquo;s eyes.</p>
