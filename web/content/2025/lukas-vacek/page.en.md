<h1>Luk&aacute;&scaron; Vacek</h1>
<h2>Feedback</h2>
<p>Spatial Design Studio<br><br>The work presents a site-specific sound installation using glass pipes as resonators of acoustic feedback. The combination of microphones, speakers and interaction with the audience creates a dynamic and unstable sound system reacting to space and movement. The installation connects listening with embodiment and creates a fluid, participatory experience in which the viewer becomes an active co-producer of the sound situation.</p>
