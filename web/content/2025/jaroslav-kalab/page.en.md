<h2>You Must Watch This</h2>
<p>Video Studio</p>
<p>The work discusses the relationship between the real and virtual worlds &ndash; the relationship between people and the online environment that is intimately connected with our every-day lives. The artist focuses on that moment when being online ceases to be of any help and benefit to our life and work, and rather starts to manipulate our time and conduct, making us its victims.</p>
