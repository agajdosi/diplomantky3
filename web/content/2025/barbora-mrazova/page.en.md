<h2>Ryžovna: A Dialogue Between Suburban Development, Landscape and Sustainable Future</h2>
<p>Environment Studio<br><br>The work focuses on the former village of Ryžovna (now gone) and the relationships between its various actors. Using field research, available sources and interviews, it notes its possible futures and identifies some key themes and challenges. Based on this research, it develops a programme proposal and facilitates an encounter between the actors. It presents an installation of the artefacts created during this encounter and a video essay reflecting the research process, its tools and the documented perspectives.</p>
