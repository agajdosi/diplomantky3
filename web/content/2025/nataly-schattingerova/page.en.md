<h1>Nataly Sch&auml;ttingerov&aacute;</h1>
<h2>Empty Armour</h2>
<p>Painting Studio 2</p>
<p>The work deconstructs the romantic ideal of knighthood as formed by pop culture and personal experience. The mid-size paintings and charcoal drawings combine knightly symbolism with modern features. The outcome is a visual dialogue between the past and present which reflects on the topic of identity, family expectations and personal fascination as part of a universal story about finding oneself.</p>
